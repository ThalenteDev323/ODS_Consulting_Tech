Core Capability

IBM Planning Analytics (TM1)

Planning Analytics is the engine behind serious financial planning — budgeting, forecasting, and multi-dimensional modelling at scale. We design workspace models that mirror how your finance team actually plans, not a generic template bolted onto your data.

Every build starts with your existing process, not the software's defaults — so adoption feels natural instead of forced.

  • Model design and build
  • Migration from legacy or spreadsheet-based planning
  • Workflow and workspace configuration
  • Integration with source systems
  • Performance tuning for large or complex cubes

Core Capability

Microsoft Power BI

Power BI is where your data becomes a story people actually understand. We build dashboards that connect directly to your source systems — including Planning Analytics — so insight doesn't wait for month-end.

Reports are built to be self-serve: once we hand over, your team can filter, drill down and extend them without calling us every time.

  • Row-level security setup
  • Training so your team can extend reports after handover

Core Capability

Data Centralisation & Automation

Most finance teams lose hours every month to copy-pasting between spreadsheets and systems that don't talk to each other. We centralise your data sources and automate the workflows that connect them, using SQL, Python and RPA where it genuinely saves time.

The goal isn't automation for its own sake — it's freeing your team to spend their time analysing numbers, not assembling them.

  • Centralised data warehousing
  • Automated data pipelines and refreshes
  • SQL and Python-based workflow automation
  • Reduced manual reconciliation and copy-paste work
Talk to us about automation

Training & Change Management

New tools only pay off if people actually use them. We run hands-on onboarding, live coaching sessions and build video libraries your team can revisit long after go-live, so adoption doesn't quietly fade a few weeks in.

We tailor training to role — a budget holder and a model builder need very different things from the same system.

  • Role-based onboarding programmes
  • Live coaching and workshops
  • On-demand video libraries
  • Change management support through rollout

User Support Centre

Models and dashboards need upkeep — new users, changing source data, the odd broken formula. Our support centre gives you a reliable, SLA-based line to real people who already know your setup, instead of starting from scratch with a new consultant every time.

Fast turnaround is the point: small issues get fixed before they become the reason a tool falls out of use.

  • SLA-based response times
  • Support from consultants who know your build
  • Proactive guidance, not just ticket queues
  • Coverage for both Planning Analytics and Power BI
