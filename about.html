About ODS

ODS Consulting began its journey in 2021, founded by a team with over 16 years of experience in financial performance and analytics.

ODS Consulting is a premium Business Performance Management (BPM) consultancy that empowers finance teams to make smarter, data-driven decisions through intuitive, integrated analytics solutions — delivered with unmatched service, care, and expert support.

From our base in Umhlanga Ridge, we deliver IBM Planning Analytics (TM1) and Microsoft Power BI solutions for finance teams across South Africa and beyond — and we stay with you long after go-live, walking alongside your team from first conversation to confident, everyday use.

Our Story

Why we exist.

In 2021, a team with over 16 years in financial performance and analytics set out to change how finance teams experience their tools.

The business was born from a clear observation: while many consulting firms excelled in technical proficiency, they often failed to connect their tools to the day-to-day realities of finance professionals.

ODS set out to bridge this gap by focusing not only on tools like IBM Planning Analytics but also on the end-users — the finance teams who needed intuitive, reliable support in their reporting workflows.

Our principle: understand the real problem first, then bring the right platform to it — never the other way round.

What sets us apart

We differ from other consulting agencies due to our ability to integrate strategy with technology.
